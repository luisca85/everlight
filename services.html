04 Common Questions
How do we secure our wedding date?

A 30% booking retainer and signed agreement are required to secure your date. Once confirmed, your date is exclusively reserved for you.

Do you travel outside of Melbourne?

Yes. Travel within Melbourne is included in all collections. Weddings outside the 20km radius are charged at a per-kilometre rate.

What happens if we're running behind on the wedding day?

We allow flexibility within the scheduled coverage time where possible. If additional time is needed and available, it can be added on the day or in advance.

How many photos will we receive?

This varies depending on the collection and coverage time, but typically:

  • 4-hour coverage: 200+ edited images
  • 6-hour coverage: 400+ edited images
All final images are carefully curated and edited to reflect your day in a natural, timeless style.

Do you offer both photography and video together?

Yes. We offer combined photo + film collections designed to create a cohesive storytelling experience of your wedding day.

What is included in the wedding film?

Our cinematic films focus on emotional storytelling, including key moments such as ceremony highlights, couple interactions, and selected audio (vows or speeches when available depending on coverage).

Do you shoot full wedding coverage (getting ready to reception)?

Full-day storytelling is available in our Signature Experience and combined collections. Shorter coverage focuses on the most meaningful parts of the day.

When will we receive our photos and films?

Standard delivery is typically within 3–5 weeks for photography and 4–6 weeks for films, depending on the season. Each gallery is carefully edited and delivered in high resolution online.

Can we request additional coverage time on the day?

Yes, if your schedule allows and availability permits, additional coverage can be added at an hourly rate.

Do you help with planning the timeline?

Yes. We provide guidance on photography timing to ensure we capture key moments in the best light and without unnecessary stress.

What if we feel awkward in front of the camera?

Most couples feel this way initially. We guide you naturally throughout the day so you never feel posed or forced. The focus is always on real interaction, not stiff posing.

Can we customise our package?

Yes. While our collections are designed to be complete, we can adjust coverage and add-ons depending on your wedding needs.
